This week was a slower week for StudySync. Work was particularly busy, and I wasn't able to dedicate as much time to the project as I had originally planned.

After completing the initial Review Guide implementation and evaluation last week, my next goal was to begin developing the Flashcards study tool. Unfortunately, I wasn't able to make much progress on that feature this week.

<!--more-->

# **What I Worked On**

Most of my progress this week involved reviewing where I left off with the Review Guide and thinking through how to approach Flashcards.

I want to reuse the existing Gemini API integration while creating a different type of study experience focused on active recall rather than summarization.

# **Challenges**

My biggest impediment this week was time. Between work responsibilities and coursework, I had less time available for development than expected.

I'm realizing that even with a relatively small project, managing development time alongside other commitments can be challenging.

# **Next Steps**

My plan is to spend time working on Flashcards over the weekend. I'd like to get the initial generation functionality working and begin building the interface for navigating between questions and revealing answers.

# **Reflection**

One thing I'm learning from this project is the importance of setting realistic weekly goals and adjusting when other responsibilities take priority.

Although I didn't accomplish as much as I had hoped this week, I have a clear next step and can pick up development without needing to rethink the overall direction of StudySync.
