## What I Worked On

My project is beginning to take shape around three main phases:

1. **Career Exploration** — Research potential computer science career paths and compare them with my interests, skills, and goals.
2. **Professional Preparation** — Update my resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio based on what I learn.
3. **Technical Portfolio Project** — Select and begin a larger technical project that supports the career direction I choose.

I also began updating this website so that I can use it throughout the semester to document my progress and eventually showcase my work.

## Website Progress

This week I:

- Updated the structure and content of my homepage
- Began improving my professional introduction
- Added information about my current skills and interests
- Created a section describing my semester project
- Started a dedicated area for weekly project updates

## Next Steps

Next, I plan to begin researching several possible computer science career paths. I want to look beyond job titles and understand the day-to-day work, technical skills, employer expectations, and current job market for each path.

That research will help me narrow my focus before making larger changes to my resume, portfolio, and technical projects.
